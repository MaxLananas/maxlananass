Grève Pétition 2026 — project file_□×

Petition & mobilization website

In October 2026, lyceens across Guadeloupe mobilized over heat in classrooms, crumbling buildings, canteens, missing teachers and endless schedules. In my lyceum I organized a mobilization of 950 students and built greve-petition.pages.dev to collect and publish our demands: 600 signatures in a few days.

Representatives were received by the rector Gabriele Fioni and by Ary Chalus, president of the Regional Council. Guadeloupe 1ère, Canal 10 and Libération covered the movement. Beyond the code, it was an extremely enriching experience in organization, communication with the mairie, the Region, the rectorat — and in getting closer to the students themselves.

Discuss a project

Independent interface redesign · fictitious demonstration data · not an official login service

What the project does

  • Demands collected and published: heat, buildings, canteen, staffing, schedules
  • Signature counter and public support wall
  • Press archive: Guadeloupe 1ère, Canal 10, Libération

Getting started

Open the site, read the demands, sign, share. The whole revendication list lives there.

Scope, limitations and attribution

A personal student initiative; signature counts are as declared by the site; not an official union or institutional platform.
