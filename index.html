001 · Profile

Who is on the survey?

Identity

I’m MaxLananas — a Minecraft builder and developer working between two grids: the voxel grid of a 1:1 build, and the logic grid of the code that powers it.

Most of my construction work happens through BuildTheEarth and partner projects, where a single monument is a shared, credited effort — a real place translated block by block with elevation data, WorldEdit precision and a lot of patience.

Alongside the blocks, I build the tools of the trade: published Minecraft client mods and plugins, shaders, and interface work. I care about the invisible stuff — structure, attribution, honest scope — as much as the finished screenshot.
